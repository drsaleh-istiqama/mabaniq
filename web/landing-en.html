Three problems every developer lives with on every new project

With every project the files and chats multiply, and the single truth gets harder to reach: who booked what, who paid how much, and which project needs attention today.

1

Scattered bookings and collections

Spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and photos of receipts: the booking lives in one place, the payment in another, and bank reconciliation is a manual month-end job.

2

A slow-selling project is noticed too late

A sales slowdown only shows when someone reviews the numbers by hand; pricing, campaigns and brokers are decided without an early signal.

3

The customer cannot see their unit or documents

They ask about construction progress, their contract, receipts and the next instalment — and get a phone call every time.

Mabaniq puts all three in one place: live inventory, collections tied to the contract, an app for the customer, and a dashboard that tells management what needs a decision today.

Features, with real screenshots

Everything shown here runs today on the demo environment and is covered by automated tests. The data is sample data.

Dashboard: what needs your decision today

The dashboard does not just show numbers; it proposes decisions ordered by priority and financial impact: an expected cash gap in a project's escrow account, a payment certificate the progress evidence does not support, a customer who needs rescheduling, or a segment worth repricing.

  • Sales, collections, cash gap and available inventory at a glance
  • Every decision carries its source (cash radar, progress verification, default model, dynamic pricing) and its status
  • Approving a decision changes the data and is written to the audit log

Inventory, pricing and plans

The building map floor by floor, every unit with its status (available, reserved, sold) and type, and below it segment performance: relative demand per type and view with the pricing engine's recommendation.

  • Floor plans and unit-type drawings are uploaded as hash-verified documents, with unit position markers
  • Dynamic pricing proposes bounded adjustments on high-demand segments
  • Clicking a unit opens its card and its position on the plan

Quotes, contracts and documents on the developer's letterhead

A numbered quote with an interest-free payment plan converts to a booking in one click, then identity verification, the signed contract, and an invoice and receipt for every instalment and payment — all PDF with your letterhead, logo, stamp, a verification code and a QR.

  • Hijri first, Western digits, an explicit payment schedule, and a public verification page per document with no personal data
  • Delivered by print, e-mail attachment or a signed WhatsApp link
  • Documents are never stored as files; they are generated from the database on demand and the registry keeps their hash

Customer app

A mobile web app the customer signs into: My unit (construction progress, photos, expected handover), Payments (instalment schedule and the next one due), Maintenance, Resale with the developer's approval, and More (contract, invoices, receipts, plans).

  • The customer sees their floor plan with their own unit marked
  • Password change is enforced on first sign-in; sign out of all devices in one tap
  • Personal-data export and privacy requests straight from the app

Collections and cash flow

A monthly cash forecast per escrow account with its drivers and the expected peak shortfall, and the list of customers at risk of default with a rescheduling proposal before they fall behind, not after.

  • Ageing of arrears and the list of overdue instalments by customer and unit
  • Late payment never becomes revenue; it is recorded as a charity obligation under the contract's charity clause and can be waived
  • Invoices and VAT, bank reconciliation by statement import, and an escrow account per project

Marketing and the ad studio

A board that classifies every project from its live data (launch, steady, slow-selling, nearly sold out, sold out) with signals and suggested actions, plus campaigns with an interest-free offer and a tracking code that measures incoming leads and cost per lead.

  • An ad studio with six templates and three sizes built from the project's data and your letterhead, with programmatic Arabic text and no people
  • The platform refuses any interest-bearing offer
  • Marketing alerts from market movement are recorded automatically

Reports and analytics

Ten ready reports one click away (portfolio, sales performance, lead funnel, quotes, brokers, collections and arrears, cash flow, inventory, construction, handover) with parameters and charts, and a report builder on approved datasets with no SQL.

  • CSV and PDF export with natively drawn charts
  • Save reports and schedule them by e-mail
  • Journal export in ERPNext format

Security and governance

Every action is a line in a hash-chained audit log nobody can edit; identity data is encrypted in the database; and every developer is isolated at the database row level, not in application code.

  • Ready roles (general manager, sales, finance, engineer, investor, broker, customer) — each sees only what concerns it
  • Two-factor authentication with recovery codes, sessions per device with remote revocation, and Google sign-in for existing accounts
  • Documented security headers, no inline scripts and no external hosts

Frequently asked questions

Is the platform ready to use now?

The platform runs on a cloud demo environment with sample data, and every feature on this page is covered by automated tests. Live operation for a developer starts after the demo meeting and the set-up of its data (projects, units, payment plans, letterhead).

Do I need to install anything?

No. Mabaniq is a web application that runs in the browser on desktop and mobile, for staff, customers and brokers. The private copy is deployed on the company's servers with ready containers.

How is my company's data isolated from other developers?

By row-level security in the database: the engine itself prevents any query from seeing another developer's rows, independent of application code. The private copy gives you a fully separate database.

Does the platform support Islamic finance?

Islamic finance only: no interest in any payment plan or marketing offer, and the platform refuses interest-bearing wording. Late payment is handled by a charity clause (a donation to a charity, not revenue for the developer); its final wording is approved by the company's Sharia advisor.

Are documents issued under my company's identity?

Yes. Quotes, contracts, invoices and receipts are issued as PDF with your letterhead, logo, stamp and authorised signature, and every document carries a verification code and a QR to a public verification page that shows no personal data.

What does the customer see?

A mobile web app: their unit and its construction progress, their instalment schedule and next payment, their contract, invoices and receipts, their floor plan and unit position, maintenance requests after handover, and owners' association votes.

Does it integrate with the accounting system?

Journals are exported in ERPNext format, and reports as CSV and PDF. Other integrations (an Omani payment gateway, WhatsApp Business, the company's e-mail) are enabled at go-live with the company's own accounts.

What language is the platform in?

Arabic first, right to left, with an English version of the shell, the sign-in screen and the customer app. Dates are Hijri then Gregorian, digits are Western.

Where is the data hosted?

In the subscription model on managed cloud hosting with a PostgreSQL database and a rehearsed backup-restore drill; in the private copy on the company's own infrastructure. Hosting and backup specifications are presented in the demo meeting.
