Frequently asked questions

Is the platform ready now?

It runs on a cloud demo environment with sample data and every feature is covered by automated tests; live operation starts after the demo meeting and your data set-up.

Do I need to install anything?

No; a web application for staff, customers and brokers. The private copy is deployed on your servers with ready containers.

How is my data isolated?

By row-level security in the database: the engine prevents seeing another developer's rows. The private copy is a separate database.

Does it support Islamic finance?

Islamic finance only; no interest in any plan or offer, and late payment is handled by a charity clause whose wording your Sharia advisor approves.

Are documents in my identity?

Yes: your letterhead, logo, stamp and authorised signature, plus a verification code and QR to a public page with no personal data.

What does the customer see?

Their unit and progress, instalments, contract, invoices and receipts, floor plan, maintenance, and owners' association votes.

Does it integrate with accounting?

Journal export in ERPNext format and CSV/PDF reports; a payment gateway, WhatsApp Business and company e-mail are enabled at go-live with your accounts.
