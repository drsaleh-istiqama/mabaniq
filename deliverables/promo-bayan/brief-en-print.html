Dashboard

What needs your decision today
  • Four indicators: sales, collections, projected liquidity gap, available inventory
  • A decision box ranked by priority and financial impact, with the source of each decision
  • The project cycle and your six projects in the side bar
  • An assistant that answers from your live data

Inventory and plans

Masahat Al Khoudh on a live plan
  • 43 units (20 shops and 23 offices) with their states on the building plan; units kept for lease are marked
  • Segment performance: relative demand per type and view, and the pricing recommendation
  • Your real plans: the ground floor with shop markers, and the three brochures
  • The customer sees their own floor and their own unit marker only

Leasing

The leasing portfolio: contracts, instalments, renewals
  • Kept units with their contracts: tenant, annual rent, instalment frequency
  • Quarterly or monthly instalments, and a late one shows at once
  • A renewal alert ninety days before a contract ends
  • Occupancy, annual rent and arrears as live indicators

Quotes and contracts

From a quote to a signed contract
  • A numbered quote on a non-interest plan (milestones, 60/40, murabaha), with a validity and a discount ceiling per role
  • It becomes a booking in one click at the quoted price
  • Identity check, then a contract sealed by a hash and signed by the customer from their phone
  • Quote, contract, invoice and receipt as PDF on your letterhead, with a verification code and a QR

Customer app

My unit · Payments · Service · Documents
  • Construction progress, photos and the expected handover date
  • The instalment schedule, online payment and receipts
  • The signed contract, documents and the unit plan
  • After handover: service requests, and resale with the developer's approval

Collections and finance

Liquidity radar and default risk
  • A monthly forecast of each escrow account, the peak shortfall and its cause
  • Customers at risk of default, with a rescheduling suggestion before the delay
  • Arrears by age, bank reconciliation, invoices and VAT
  • Lateness is a donation under the contract, not revenue: Islamic finance only

Construction and handover

Actual progress against sales
  • Payment certificates are approved at the percentage the evidence supports (photos and minutes)
  • Progress is compared with sales per project (Masahat Al Khoudh at 64% in the demo data)
  • Handover with an appointment, a certificate and a defects list, and never before the handover instalment
  • Owners' association: a budget and votes weighted by unit area

Marketing and ads

Which project needs action today
  • A board that classes each project from its live data: launch, weak sales, steady, near complete, complete
  • Campaigns with a structural offer only (the platform rejects any offer with interest) and a tracking code that measures cost per lead
  • An ad studio: 6 templates in 3 sizes on your real renders, with no people
  • Daily marketing alerts from market movement

Reports

Ten ready reports and a builder that needs no SQL
  • Portfolio, sales, funnel, quotes, brokers, collections, liquidity, inventory, construction, handover
  • A report builder over approved datasets with filters, grouping and charts
  • CSV and PDF export on your letterhead, saved and scheduled by email
  • Lender and investor reports with their own permissions

Security and governance

What reassures management
  • Every action is a line in an audit log chained by hash, which nobody can edit
  • Two-factor sign-in, sessions per device, remote sign-out, graded lockout
  • Roles and permissions on the server; encrypted personal data; each developer isolated in the database
  • Public verification of any document by its code, with no personal data shown

What it takes to go live

Unit schedules, prices and rents · existing lease contracts · logo, stamp and signature at print resolution · images of the other projects · an SMTP mailbox and a domain · persistent storage for attachments · approval of the Sharia and legal wording

Two ways to work together: a subscription on Jadwa's hosted platform, or a private copy on Bayan's infrastructure. We suggest a live demonstration on the Masahat Al Khoudh data.
