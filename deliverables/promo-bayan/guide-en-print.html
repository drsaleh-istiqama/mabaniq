About this guide

The platform as Bayan Oman would use it today

This guide shows Mabaneek, the real estate development platform from Jadwa for Studies and Development, loaded with Bayan Oman's published portfolio: Masahat Al Khoudh, Al Shabab Business Centre, Alaa Business Avenue, MQ Residences, Mabelah Gateway and Al Khuwair Square, with the plans and renders from your three brochures and your letterhead on every document.

  • Every screenshot comes from the working platform (version 0.15.0) after loading your published data: 317 units in 6 projects and 29 plans and renders.
  • Prices, rents, sales ratios, progress percentages, tenants and buyers are illustrative. Bayan does not publish prices or rents; the real figures will run through the same logic.
  • Your portfolio is mostly commercial, so the guide starts from leasing (contracts, quarterly instalments, arrears, renewals, occupancy) next to sales, not from residential sales alone.
  • The product is Arabic-first and right-to-left, so the screenshots show the Arabic interface. The English switch covers navigation labels; project names, units and documents come from the server in Arabic. Each caption explains what you are looking at.
  • Nothing is promised that does not exist. What is not yet proven live, or waits on a decision from Bayan, is listed on the last two pages.
  • Finance is Islamic finance only: no interest and no interest-bearing loans. Payment plans are murabaha or instalments, and a late-payment charge is a charitable donation, never revenue.
Why now

Three problems of a developer that sells and leases at the same time

A commercial portfolio means hundreds of offices and shops. Some are sold, some are kept and leased, and all of them need one version of the truth: who occupies what, who paid how much, and which contract ends next month.

1. Leases and quarterly instalments scattered across sheets

A contract in one file, a quarterly payment in a bank statement, a renewal discovered after the contract has ended. Arrears surface only when someone reviews the sheet by hand.

2. One building, sold and leased together

At Masahat Al Khoudh some shops and offices are for sale and others are kept for rent. Without one system the states blur, and the signal about price and demand on each segment is lost.

3. A group with more than one entity

Bayan Investment House and Bayan Oman each need their own data and their own letterhead, without separate systems and duplicated accounts.

Mabaneek brings the three into one place: a live leasing portfolio with its contracts and instalments, sale inventory on the real plans, and isolated developers inside a single platform.

Sign-in and identity

Secure sign-in under your company's name, and one entity per company in the group

Users pick the developer, Bayan Oman, and sign in with a password or with the company's Google account. Accounts are never created from outside: an identity is matched to an account that an administrator created. The same developer list is where other entities of the group live, if Bayan Investment House wants a separate portfolio with its own letterhead.

  • Ready-made roles: general manager, sales, finance, engineer, investor, broker and customer. Each role sees only what concerns it.
  • Two-factor authentication (TOTP) with recovery codes, sessions listed per device and ended remotely, and graded lockout against guessing.
  • Each entity in a group is a developer isolated row by row in the database (row-level security), not merely in the application.
Dashboard

What needs your decision today

The dashboard does not only show numbers. It proposes decisions ranked by priority and financial impact: a projected liquidity gap in a project's escrow account, a payment certificate the progress evidence does not support, a customer who needs rescheduling, or an office segment worth repricing.

  • Portfolio: 317 units in 6 projects (illustrative data on the areas published in your brochures).
  • Liquidity: a monthly forecast of each escrow account with the expected peak shortfall and its cause.
  • Today's decisions: each one carries its source (liquidity radar, progress check, default model, dynamic pricing) and its status.

The tenant and buyer journey, 1

A quote for an office with a non-interest payment plan

A sales officer issues a numbered quote for an available unit with a payment plan (tied to progress milestones, 60/40, or murabaha through an Islamic bank) and a discount within the officer's authority. A quote does not reserve the unit, and it becomes a booking in one click at the quoted price.

  • The quote PDF carries your letterhead and logo: Hijri date first, Western digits, an explicit payment table, and a verification code with a QR.
  • A fixed validity, and a discount ceiling by role (sales officer 2%, holders of the decide permission up to 15%). Every quote is in the audit log.
  • The quote shown was issued by the platform for unit MSK-M-203 to an imaginary customer.
The tenant and buyer journey, 2

Booking, identity check and the signed contract

A booking creates the instalment schedule at once. The identity check (KYC) records the document and rates the risk. The contract is issued with its text sealed by a hash; the customer signs from the app or in person, and the account is created automatically.

  • The contract carries the donation clause (a charitable donation in place of a late penalty), marked for approval by a Sharia adviser until Bayan adopts it.
  • No handover without a bank release letter, and no escrow release without an approved payment certificate: the rules sit in the core, not in custom.
  • Every document (contract, invoice, receipt) is generated from the database on request and never stored as a file, and anyone can verify it publicly by its code.
The tenant and buyer journey, 3

The invoice and the receipt on your letterhead

Every instalment due produces an invoice (a tax invoice where VAT applies, as it does on commercial sales), and every payment produces a receipt. Delivery to the customer is print, email with an attachment, or a signed WhatsApp link valid for 30 days.

  • Numbering runs in sequence for each year, amounts use Western digits, and dates show Hijri first and then Gregorian.
  • The logo, stamp and authorised signature are uploaded once in Document identity and appear on every document.
  • The two samples below were issued by the platform for the first customer at Masahat Al Khoudh (illustrative data).
Customer app

The customer sees their unit, their documents and their place on the plan

A phone web app the customer signs in to with their own account: My unit (construction progress, expected date, progress photos), Payments (the instalment schedule and online payment), Service (requests after handover), Resale (the secondary market with the developer's approval), and More (contract, invoices, receipts, plans, and voting in the owners' association).

  • The customer sees the plan of their floor with their own unit marked, the plan of their unit type, and the public renders.
  • Changing the password is mandatory on first sign-in, and the customer can sign out of all other devices.
  • Personal-data export and privacy requests are available in the app.
Collections and finance

Liquidity radar and default risk, with a donation in place of a penalty

A monthly forecast of cash flows for each escrow account, with the drivers (payment certificates, scheduled instalments) and the expected peak shortfall. A list of customers at risk of default with their scores, and a rescheduling suggestion before the delay, not after it.

  • Arrears by age (30, 60 and 90 days) and a list of late instalments by customer and unit; lease arrears sit in the leasing screen.
  • Lateness never produces a penalty as revenue. It is recorded as a donation under the contract's clause (1% a month after 15 days, a provisional value to be reviewed with the Sharia adviser), and it can be waived.
  • Invoices and VAT (5% on commercial sales), bank reconciliation by importing a statement, and an escrow account per project: no release without an approved certificate, and 10% retention until handover.
Construction and payment certificates

Actual progress against sales

Payment certificates (IPCs) are approved at the percentage the evidence supports (photos and minutes). When a claim exceeds the evidence, the platform proposes approving the supported percentage and estimates the overpayment avoided. The schedule is compared with sales, so a project selling faster than it is built, or the reverse, shows up.

  • Masahat Al Khoudh stands at 64% progress in the demo data, with about half of its shops sold; Al Shabab Business Centre is at the start, with its building permit 2024/1593 printed in the brochure.
  • Alaa Business Avenue is at 36%: the structural stage drives its liquidity peak on the dashboard.
  • Each certificate approval needs its own permission (approve_ipc) and leaves an audit line.
Handover and the owners' association

MQ Residences near handover, and Al Khuwair Square completed with an owners' association

Handover is scheduled with an appointment and documented with a certificate, a defects list, meter readings and keys, and it cannot complete before the handover instalment is paid. After completion the owners' association takes over the budget and area-weighted voting from the customer app.

  • MQ Residences (residential, 92% progress in the demo data): handover dates and open defects.
  • Al Khuwair Square (completed and handed over): the owners' association, its budget and its votes, with its retained units leased.
  • Facilities management and title transfer sit in the neighbouring tabs.
Reports and analytics

Ten ready reports and a report builder that needs no SQL

Ready reports at a click (portfolio, sales performance, lead funnel, quotes, brokers, collections and arrears, liquidity, inventory, construction, handover) with parameters and charts. A report builder over approved datasets with filters and grouping, CSV and PDF export with charts, and saved and scheduled reports by email.

  • Charts are drawn inside the platform with no external library, and in PDF by the same engine.
  • Lender and investor reports have their own permissions; occupancy, annual rent and arrears are indicators in the leasing screen.
  • The two reports attached to this package were exported by the platform on Bayan Oman letterhead.
Marketing

The marketing board: which project needs action today

The board classifies each project from live data (sales pace over 30 and 90 days against plan, relative demand on each segment, stalled leads, progress) as launch, weak sales, steady, near complete or complete, with signals, suggested actions, and New campaign and Ad buttons.

  • A campaign carries a structural offer only (deferral, instalments, fee waivers, bundles). The platform rejects any offer that involves interest.
  • Each campaign has a tracking code that measures incoming leads and the cost per lead; sending by email or WhatsApp is logged.
  • In this demo data MQ Residences is classed weak sales (near handover with unsold units) and Alaa steady, because its illustrative sales run ahead of plan. The class is computed from live data and moves with it.
Ad studio

A ready ad on the real Masahat Al Khoudh render

Six templates (teaser, launch, special offer, last units, construction progress, ready to occupy) in three sizes. Arabic text is composed in code over the background image you choose, here the Masahat render from the brochure, with your logo and contact details and no people.

  • The two ads below were produced by the studio inside the platform (launch template and last units, square size).
  • An ad is stored as PNG and PDF, tied to a campaign and sent to its audience.
  • Marketing alerts are logged daily from market movement (stalled leads, slowing pace, a chance to raise a price).
Governance and security

An audit log that cannot be erased, and public verification of every document

Every action in the platform is a line in an audit log chained by hash, which nobody can edit. Personal data is encrypted in the database, and each entity is isolated by rows at database level, not at application level.

  • Users and roles: add, change the email, end sessions, reset two-factor.
  • Document identity and stamp: legal name, commercial registration, tax number, address, logo, stamp and signature.
  • The public /verify page shows the type, number, date and content hash with no personal data.

What Bayan needs to go live

  2. Existing lease contractsTenants, the start and end of each contract and the instalments paid, imported in one pass so arrears and renewals show from day one.
  3. Logo, stamp and signature at print resolutionTransparent PNG files at high resolution; the current logo is the website's resolution only.
  4. Images and plans of the other projectsAmaken, Oman Post Qurum and Al Khoudh, Barka Hall, Masahat 1 and MQ Residences: there is not enough public material for them yet, and only the three brochures are in the platform.
  5. An SMTP mailbox and a domainTo send documents, alerts and sign-in links from Bayan's own email. Today email runs in outbox mode.
  6. WhatsApp Business (optional)Today sending uses a wa.me link from the staff member's device; automated sending needs a Business API account and a provider.
  7. Persistent storage for attachmentsUploaded plans and documents need persistent storage (a volume or S3) before first real use.
  8. Approval of the Sharia and legal wordingThe donation clause and the model sale and lease contracts, from the company's Sharia and legal advisers.
  9. Users and entitiesThe staff list and their roles, whether Bayan Investment House becomes a separate entity, and Google sign-in on the company domain if you wish.

Plainly stated

What must be said plainly

  • Prices, rents, sales ratios, progress, tenants and buyers in this guide are illustrative, built on the areas in your published brochures. Bayan publishes neither prices nor rents, and the MQ Residences units are illustrative throughout.
  • There are no images yet for Amaken, Oman Post, Barka Hall and Masahat 1. The markers on the shop plan are approximate. The Arabic name Bayan Oman is taken from your website and Instagram account.

  • Files uploaded by staff on a demo environment are temporary until persistent storage is attached; Bayan's sample images alone return automatically.

Everything else works today on the live link, and Bayan can try it with accounts of its own.
