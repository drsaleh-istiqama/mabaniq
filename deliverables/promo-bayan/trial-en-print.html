Trial guide for Mabaneek: Bayan Oman

16 Jumada al-Awwal 1448 AH · 27 October 2026 · a trial environment prepared for you

This is a trial environment prepared in your company's name: your projects (Masahat Al Khoudh, Al Shabab Business Centre, Alaa Business Avenue, MQ Residences, Mabelah Gateway, Al Khuwair Square), the plans of your brochures and your letterhead, so you can try how the leasing portfolio, sales, collections, handover and marketing are run.

1. How to sign in

  • Open https://mabaneek.com, then Sign in (or https://mabaneek.com/login directly).
  • From the Developer list choose Bayan Oman.
  • Type a username from the table below and the temporary password (it reaches you in a separate message, never in this guide).
  • You will be asked for a new password of your own (10 characters or more, with letters and digits) before you continue.
UsernameRoleWhat it sees
bayan-ceoGeneral managerEverything: decisions, inventory, leasing, marketing, finance, reports, users and security
bayan-salesSalesInventory, leads, quotes, bookings and contracts, brokers and commissions, project marketing
bayan-financeFinance and collectionsLiquidity and default risk, invoices and escrow, bank reconciliation, certificates, reports, the leasing portfolio (leases and rent)

2. A suggested 15-minute tour

  • 1bayan-ceoDashboard: the decision box ranked by priority and financial impact; click any decision to see why.
  • 2bayan-ceoInventory and pricing, choose Masahat Al Khoudh, then the Plans button: the ground floor with shop markers and your brochure plans.
  • 3bayan-financeHandover and property, then Leasing (finance and general manager permission): the contracts of the kept units, their quarterly instalments, the late one and the renewal alert. Try + Lease on the vacant unit.
  • 4bayan-salesContracts and brokers, then Quotes, then + Quote: issue a murabaha quote for an available office, open the PDF on your letterhead and turn it into a booking.
  • 5bayan-clientThe customer app: My unit (progress), Payments (the instalment schedule), More (the contract, the plans and the unit's place on them).
  • 6bayan-financeLiquidity and default risk, then Invoices and escrow: the liquidity radar, arrears, and late-payment donations in place of a penalty.
  • 7bayan-ceoProject marketing: the board that classes your projects, then the Ad studio to design an ad on the Masahat render.
  • 8bayan-ceoReports and analytics: open the Portfolio dashboard and export it as PDF or CSV.

3. Know this before you try

  • All prices, rents, sales ratios, tenants, customers and payments in this environment are illustrative, built on the areas in your published brochures. Please do not enter real customer or tenant data.
  • The developer list on the sign-in screen shows other developers that have nothing to do with you. Your data is isolated from theirs at database level, which also lets Bayan Investment House have a separate entity if you wish.
  • Email runs in outbox mode (no real email is sent yet), WhatsApp opens a wa.me link from your device, and files you upload are temporary and may be cleared when the platform is updated.
  • The platform is Islamic finance only: no plan carries interest, and lateness is a donation under the contract, not revenue.
  • After your first sign-in you choose a password of your own that nobody else knows, and you can switch on two-factor sign-in from Administration and security, then Security.
