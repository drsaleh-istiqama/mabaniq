The problem

Three problems of a developer that sells and leases at the same time
  • Leases and quarterly instalments scattered across sheets: A contract in one file, a quarterly payment in a bank statement, a renewal discovered after the contract has ended.
  • One building, sold and leased together: At Masahat Al Khoudh some shops and offices are for sale and others are kept for rent.
  • A group with more than one entity: Bayan Investment House and Bayan Oman each need their own data and their own letterhead, without separate systems and duplicated accounts.
